About us!

This is our story

We are a student-focused ticket company that makes it easier and more affordable for young people to enjoy live events. We sell tickets to concerts, festivals, parties, and other exciting events at special student-friendly prices. We believe that students should be able to experience great events without having to worry about expensive ticket prices. Our goal is to connect students with unforgettable experiences, great music, and exciting events while helping them save money. Whether you want to see your favorite artist, discover new music, or spend an amazing night with friends, we are here to help make it happen for less.
